INDOMINUS REX

The Indominus Rex is a fictional dinosaur from the movie "Jurassic World." It is a genetically modified hybrid created by the company InGen.

INDORAPTOR

Bred in secret at Lockwood Manor, the Indoraptor combines Velociraptor agility with Indominus Rex aggression. Smaller and more compact than its predecessor, it was designed as a weaponized asset rather than a park attraction.

TYRANNOSAURUS REX

The original apex predator of Jurassic Park. Even decades after her escape, the park's first T. rex remains a park icon, her roar alone enough to send guests running long before she's within striking distance.
